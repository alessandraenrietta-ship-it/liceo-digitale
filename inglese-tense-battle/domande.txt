# COME SI SCRIVE UNA DOMANDA
# --------------------------
# Ogni domanda comincia con la riga [DOMANDA] e contiene:
#
#   frase:          la frase da completare. Il buco si scrive con
#                   tre trattini bassi: ___
#                   Se i buchi sono due, nelle risposte si separano
#                   le due parti con una barra: have been / have done
#   A:              la prima risposta
#   B:              la seconda risposta
#   C:              la terza risposta
#   giusta:         la lettera della risposta giusta (A, B oppure C)
#   tempo:          il nome del tempo verbale, come compare sul sito
#   famiglia:       decide il colore della spiegazione. Scrivi una
#                   di queste tre parole:
#                     simple      (blu)
#                     continuous  (verde)
#                     perfect     (ocra)
#   domanda guida:  la domanda che aiuta a trovare il motivo
#   spiegazione:    perché la risposta è quella giusta. Le parole
#                   tra due asterischi, *così*, compaiono in grassetto
#   trappola:       (facoltativa) l'errore tipico di chi parla
#                   italiano
#   sfida:          (facoltativa) scrivi "sì" se è una domanda
#                   sfida: sul sito ha l'etichetta CHALLENGE
#
# ALTRE COSE DA SAPERE
# --------------------
# - Le righe che iniziano con # sono commenti: il sito le ignora.
# - Le righe vuote servono solo a rendere il file leggibile.
# - Le domande compaiono nell'ordine in cui sono scritte qui.
# - Se sbagli a scrivere una domanda il sito non si blocca: la
#   salta e, nella pagina delle squadre, ti dice quale rivedere.
#

[DOMANDA]
frase: In our practical lessons, I usually ___ with the lathe.
A: have worked
B: work
C: am working
giusta: B
tempo: Present simple
famiglia: simple
domanda guida: Is it a habit, a routine or a fact?
spiegazione: *usually* = a habit.

[DOMANDA]
frase: At the moment I ___ on a project about automation.
A: am working
B: work
C: was working
giusta: A
tempo: Present continuous
famiglia: continuous
domanda guida: Is it happening now?
spiegazione: *at the moment* = now, in progress.

[DOMANDA]
frase: Last year I ___ a work placement in a local company.
A: have done
B: do
C: did
giusta: C
tempo: Past simple
famiglia: simple
domanda guida: Is it finished, and do we say when?
spiegazione: *last year* = we say when. Finished.

[DOMANDA]
frase: I ___ mechanics at this school for five years.
A: have been studying
B: am studying
C: study
giusta: A
tempo: Present perfect continuous
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: Did it start in the past, and is it still going on?
spiegazione: Started five years ago and still going on: *for five years*.
trappola: Italian trap: studio da cinque anni ≠ I study / I am studying

[DOMANDA]
frase: So far, I ___ two projects with my team.
A: completed
B: am completing
C: have completed
giusta: C
tempo: Present perfect
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: Is it an experience or a result, without saying when?
spiegazione: *so far* = up to now. No finished time.

[DOMANDA]
frase: I ___ a machine when a sensor stopped working.
A: was testing
B: tested
C: have tested
giusta: A
tempo: Past continuous
famiglia: continuous
domanda guida: Was it in progress when something else happened?
spiegazione: Long action in progress (*was testing*) + short action that interrupts it (*stopped*).

[DOMANDA]
frase: How long ___ here? – For two months.
A: are you working
B: do you work
C: have you been working
giusta: C
tempo: Present perfect continuous
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: Did it start in the past, and is it still going on?
spiegazione: *How long…?* + still working here now.
trappola: Italian trap: Da quanto lavori qui? ≠ How long are you working here?

[DOMANDA]
frase: This week I ___ in the maintenance department, because my tutor is on holiday.
A: work
B: am working
C: have worked
giusta: B
tempo: Present continuous
famiglia: continuous
domanda guida: Is it happening only for a while?
spiegazione: *this week* = a temporary situation.

[DOMANDA]
frase: I ___ the safety course, so now I can use the machines.
A: already finish
B: am already finishing
C: have already finished
giusta: C
tempo: Present perfect
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: Is it an experience or a result, without saying when?
spiegazione: *already* + a result that matters now: I can use the machines.

[DOMANDA]
frase: During my placement last summer, I ___ how to use a lathe.
A: learn
B: learned
C: have learned
giusta: B
tempo: Past simple
famiglia: simple
domanda guida: Is it finished, and do we say when?
spiegazione: *last summer*: the placement is over.

[DOMANDA]
sfida: sì
frase: I ___ three job interviews this month, and it's only the 20th!
A: had
B: have had
C: was having
giusta: B
tempo: Present perfect
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: Is the time period finished?
spiegazione: *this month* is not finished yet, so we use the present perfect. Compare: *last month* I had three.

[DOMANDA]
sfida: sì
frase: How long ___ your placement? – Two months. It ended in June.
A: did it last
B: has it lasted
C: has it been lasting
giusta: A
tempo: Past simple
famiglia: simple
domanda guida: Is it finished, and do we know when?
spiegazione: *How long…?* but the placement is over (*it ended in June*). Compare with question 7: still working = present perfect continuous.

[DOMANDA]
sfida: sì
frase: When I arrived at the workshop, the technician ___ the engine, so I watched him.
A: repaired
B: has repaired
C: was repairing
giusta: C
tempo: Past continuous
famiglia: continuous
domanda guida: Was it already in progress at that moment?
spiegazione: He started before I arrived and I watched him work. *When I arrived, he repaired it* = he started after I arrived.

[DOMANDA]
sfida: sì
frase: I ___ engines all morning, and so far I ___ three.
A: have repaired / have been repairing
B: have been repairing / have repaired
C: was repairing / repaired
giusta: B
tempo: Pres. perf. continuous + pres. perfect
famiglia: perfect
domanda guida: The activity, or the result?
spiegazione: *all morning* = the activity, still going on → continuous. *three so far* = the result, a number → simple.

[DOMANDA]
sfida: sì
frase: Sorry, I ___ what you mean. Can you say it again?
A: don't understand
B: am not understanding
C: haven't been understanding
giusta: A
tempo: Present simple
famiglia: simple
domanda guida: Is it a state, not an action?
spiegazione: *understand, know, like, need, want* are states: we don't normally use them with -ing.
